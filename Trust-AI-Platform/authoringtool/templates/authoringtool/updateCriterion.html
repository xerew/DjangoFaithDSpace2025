How it works

After students complete the evaluation, their average answer weight (1–3) across all activities in the group is compared against your thresholds:

  • High — avg weight ≥ High threshold
  • Moderate — avg weight ≥ Moderate threshold
  • Low — everyone else (fallback, threshold not used)

Recommended defaults: High = 2.5, Moderate = 1.5.

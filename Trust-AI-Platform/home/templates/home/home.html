About the Platform

Inquiry-Based Learning with Trust AI Lab

Trust AI Lab supports Inquiry-Based Learning (IBL) - a student-centred pedagogical approach where learners explore, question, and discover knowledge through guided investigation rather than passive instruction.

As a teacher, you design scenarios - interactive digital experiments that guide students step by step through a scientific inquiry cycle. Each scenario can include simulations, remote labs, VR/AR experiences, adaptive evaluation, and branching paths based on student performance.

This platform was developed as part of the FAITH and DSpace projects, funded by the European Union under grant agreements No. 101135932 and No. 101086701.
